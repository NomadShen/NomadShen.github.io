I am a PhD student in the Computer Security Group at Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica (CWI Amsterdam) (the national research institute for mathematics and computer science in the Netherlands).

My research focuses on enhencing hardware security with cryptographic tools. I am particularly interested in hardware security, formal methods, and applied cryptography.

## Interested topics:
1. Privacy-preserving hardware formal verification
2. Hardware side-channel analysis
3. Secure computation protocols
